Bloom care

How to keep your arrangement fresh for longer.

01

Trim stems

Cut 1–2 cm from the base and angle the stems before placing in water.

02

Fresh water

Use clean, cool water and replace it every two days to maximize freshness.

03

Bright placement

Keep away from direct sunlight and heat to preserve bloom quality and color.
